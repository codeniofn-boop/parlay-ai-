        How stakes are sized

        Edge = P(true) × decimal odds − 1. Flat modes risk a fixed unit on every ticket with positive edge. Kelly risks Edge ÷ (decimal odds − 1) × fraction of the bankroll. Every mode caps a single ticket at of the bankroll, caps the whole week at , rounds down to the cent, and never bets a ticket with zero or negative edge.
